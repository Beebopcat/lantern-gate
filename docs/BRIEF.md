## Visual identity: "Lantern storybook HD-2D"
Deep midnight-indigo panels, antique-gold double rules with corner brackets, parchment-cream text, warm lantern-gold glow for selection and focus, serif small-caps headings, a pixel face ("Pixelify Sans", already bundled in `assets/fonts/`) for numerals and tiny labels, segmented gauges, a small glowing diamond as the selection cursor. Pixel-art characters with dark selective outlines and soft ground shadows standing in a miniature-diorama world. Shared tokens and primitives are in `jrpg-theme.css` (CSS variables `--jr-*`, classes `.jr-panel`, `.jr-title`, `.jr-btn`, `.jr-bar`, `.jr-chip`, `.jr-num`, `.jr-sprite`). Always use `var(--jr-x, fallback)`. Only the UI agent (U) may edit `jrpg-theme.css`; others use it as is and put their own styling in their own CSS file.

## Shared terminology for battles and quests (original; use consistently)
- **Ward**: each foe has a Ward meter (a few pips). **Frays**: each foe has 2 to 3 weaknesses chosen from these damage types: `blade`, `bow`, `arcane`, `flame`, `frost`, `spirit`. Hitting a hidden fray reveals it; hitting a fray removes 1 Ward per hit. At 0 Ward the foe is **Staggered**: it loses its next turn and takes +50% damage until it recovers (Ward then refills).
- **Kindle**: the party gains 1 Kindle point per round (max 5, start 1). Before choosing a command the player may kindle (up to 3 points) to multiply that command (more hits, stronger effect).
- **Lantern Surge**: optional later; a gauge filled by dealing and taking damage; skip unless time allows.
- Quests: **Chapters** for the main story, **Side stories**, and **Commissions** (board jobs); each has a recommended level, region, tracked objective, rewards.
- Classes and their three field abilities (the battle skills must map to these): warrior: Shield Wall, Cleave, Rallying Cry; summoner: Spirit Lash, Rally the Bound, Recall; mage and ranger: read their ability names from the class cards and `journey.js`.
